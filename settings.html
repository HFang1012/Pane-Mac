Tweak

Point at a file.
Fix it right there.

Double-tap the Tweak key and point at a file on your desktop, in Finder, or in an app’s Open dialog. Click it to crop, trim, cut or rewrite it, change its type, and save it back.

Tap twice
Tweak keys

Click the key to change it. Tap it twice, quickly.

Allow Pane to hear your keys

Tweak needs it to notice a double-tap, and to see which file is under your pointer in Finder and in Open dialogs. Turn on Pane in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Checking…
Good to know
  • Esc, a right-click or a click on nothing puts the pointer away.
  • Saving over a file puts the old version in the Trash, so nothing is lost.
  • Drop a file on the editor, or press ⌘O, to open any file.

Plugin

Give an app more
of your Mac. Or less.

Press the Plugin keys and click any app. A plug lands on its corner. Click the plug to share out your processor, memory, network and sound, give it a network of its own, or let it take over in Focus mode.

Plugin keys

Click the keys to change them.

Allow Pane to see your clicks

Plugin needs it so a click picks an app instead of landing in it. Turn on Pane in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Checking…
Good to know
  • Each share is your Mac in tenths. Fewer is a limit; the raspberry cells past ten are boosts.
  • Turn a share off, unplug, or quit Pane, and everything goes back.
  • Drag a rail all the way left to turn that share off.
